Legislative Context

Following the promulgation of the Constitution of Kenya 2010, whose central theme is the devolved system of Government, the Water Act 2002 was repealed giving way to the Water Act 2016. This legislative transition fundamentally changed the institutional framework for water services delivery in Kenya, redefining the mandate of water works development agencies under the new devolved governance structure.

Institutional Mandate

01

Development, maintenance and management of National Public Water Works.

02

Operate Water Works and provide water services as a Water Service Provider.

Section 68(b) — Water Act 2016
03

Provide reserve capacity for purposes of providing water services.

Section 68(c) — Water Act 2016
04

Provide Technical Services and Capacity Building to County Governments and Water Service Providers (WSPs) as may be requested.

05

Provide Technical Support to the Water Cabinet Secretary in the discharge of their functions.

How We Achieve Our Mandate

To deliver on the above mandate, the Agency pursues the following key operational objectives:

Develop Water Supply Infrastructure
Develop Sewerage Infrastructure
Enrich Stakeholder Engagement
Mainstream Cross-Cutting Issues
Enhance Institutional Capacity
Certification

TWWDA is ISO 9001:2015 certified.
