Mr. Teobald Mukundi Wambugu
Board Chairman

Mr. Teobald Mukundi Wambugu serves as the Board Chairman of Tana Water Works Development Agency, providing strategic leadership and oversight to the Agency's governance structures. He chairs the Board of Directors and ensures the Agency fulfils its mandate under the Water Act 2016, driving accountability and service delivery across the Agency's area of jurisdiction.
