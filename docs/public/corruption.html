Integrity & Accountability

Report Corruption

TWWDA is committed to zero tolerance for corruption. If you have witnessed or suspect any corrupt practice involving the Agency, its staff, or contractors, please report it through this page or any of the channels listed.

Home› Report Corruption

Corruption Reporting

Why Report

Your report helps protect public resources

Corruption in public water infrastructure diverts funds meant to bring clean water to communities. Every report — however small it may seem — contributes to accountability and better service delivery for millions of Kenyans.

Confidentiality
Your identity and personal details are handled with strict confidentiality. You may also report anonymously.
Protection from Retaliation
Whistleblowers are protected under Kenyan law. Retaliation against a person who reports in good faith is prohibited.
Timely Action
All reports are reviewed by the Internal Audit and Assurance unit and escalated appropriately within the Agency.
